Investor and validation view

Local AI setup and support for businesses that want more control.

SANAD would assess a business use case, install a local AI system, hand over the working environment and offer maintenance if the customer wants it.

Problem

Cloud AI works well for many businesses. Some businesses still have reasons to keep certain AI work local, including confidential documents, repeated high-volume use, service continuity or a preference to control the hardware and software stack.

What customers are buying

A working local AI setup tied to a real business task. That includes the model runtime, interface, document access, configuration, testing and handover.

Why they would pay SANAD

Most buyers will not want to assemble and maintain the stack themselves. SANAD does the setup, documents it and remains available for support.

Who is likely to buy

The market should be defined by the work, not by a broad industry label.

Useful indicators

  • Confidential or internal documents
  • Frequent AI use
  • A workflow that can run locally
  • Concern about cloud outages or changing prices
  • No internal team to build and maintain the system

Who should not buy

If ChatGPT, Claude or another cloud service already solves the problem cheaply and safely enough, local infrastructure adds cost and support work for little benefit.

Revenue model

Most early revenue would come from assessment and installation. Monthly care plans provide recurring revenue without turning the system into a rental product.

How the business could start

Core team

One technical lead can handle assessment, build and support. A commercial lead can handle sales and customer relationships. Security, networking, legal and accounting work can be contracted when needed.

Sales process

Qualify the use case first. Then demonstrate a working example, scope the job, install it, test it with the customer and hand it over. Offer support after the system is working.

Ownership model

The claim that customers own the system only works if SANAD avoids unnecessary lock-in.

Customer data stays separate

Business documents and backups should not be trapped inside one interface or runtime.

Components can be replaced

Ollama, llama.cpp, MLX or another runtime can be changed if licensing, pricing or support changes.

Support is optional

Stopping the care plan should not disable the system. SANAD is paid to maintain it, not to unlock it.

Risks to test before investing

  • Cloud services may be good enough.This is the biggest commercial risk. SANAD needs customers with a clear reason to run locally.
  • Local models may not match the quality customers expect.Each use case needs testing before hardware is sold or installed.
  • Support can become labour-heavy.The business needs standard builds and clear support boundaries or monthly care fees will be eaten by service time.
  • Security expectations can exceed a small provider's capability.Higher-risk work may require specialist partners, formal controls or a narrower scope.

Why run AI locally

Local AI makes sense when control, cost or continuity is more important than the convenience of a standard cloud subscription.

Keep selected data local

Approved documents can stay on business-controlled hardware.

Keep working during a cloud outage

Local workloads can continue even if an external AI service is unavailable.

Avoid per-token charges for local work

Once the hardware is in place, local inference does not carry a cloud token bill for each request.

Change the software later

The setup can be designed so the model runtime or interface can be replaced without rebuilding the whole environment.
