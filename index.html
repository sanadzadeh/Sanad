Why businesses are considering private AI

Valuable uses of AI often involve confidential data, internal documents and regulated work. Private AI creates a controlled place for that work.

Use cases

External requirements, private business data

Many businesses need to apply external rules to internal documents they cannot freely share. We bring those sources together with controlled access and review.

Use case: independent real estate agency

Use private AI to work with leases, property records and agency procedures without routinely sending customer data to public tools.

  • Search property procedures and guidance
  • Draft routine correspondence from templates
  • Summarise and locate information across documents

We set access boundaries and review points for sensitive records.

Use case: neighbourhood clinic

Use private AI for administrative procedures, forms and communications while keeping patient material within a controlled environment.

  • Search administrative policies and guidance
  • Draft routine non-clinical communications
  • Help staff navigate forms and procedures

We keep clinical work outside the initial scope and build in privacy and professional review.

Use case: accounting and bookkeeping practice

Use private AI with financial records, workpapers and tax procedures without routinely moving client data into public tools.

  • Search internal procedures and guidance
  • Compare checklists with current source material
  • Draft routine requests and summaries

We control source updates and professional review of conclusions.
